FOLLOW THE SCRIPT LINE BY LINE. Whatever it names — any room, any kind of home, any style, any era, any colour, any material, any piece of furniture, any light — show exactly that. These notes set quality, never subject.

WHEN A LINE NAMES A TREND, show it clearly and recognisably, the way the viewer has seen it in real homes, catalogues and social media, so they know it in a second. Show it inside a real, lived-in room, not as a product shot on a plain background.

WHEN A LINE NAMES WHAT REPLACES A TREND, OR WHAT TO DO INSTEAD, show that just as clearly.

HOMES: any kind — apartments and houses, small and large, old and new, city and suburban, European and American, modest and luxurious. Rooms furnished and styled unless the line describes an empty or bare room.

LIGHT: whatever suits the line — bright daylight, soft morning light, golden afternoon, evening lamplight, cool or warm. Natural and believable.

DETAIL: when a line names a specific finish, fabric, fixture or object, move in close on it.

KEEP OUT: any text, captions, numbers or graphics. No on-screen words, labels, logos or signs.

TONE: clear, confident and true to life — every trend instantly recognisable.
