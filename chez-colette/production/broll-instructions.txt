Chez Colette. Warm, welcoming French homes, professionally decorated. Elegant Haussmann-style city apartments, present day.

THE SCRIPT CHOOSES THE ROOM — sitting room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, hallway, storage, studio, whole flat. Follow it. These notes set style, never subject.

SHOW: every room fully furnished, styled and lived-in — never empty or bare. Tall windows, linen curtains to the floor, herringbone parquet, cream walls, mouldings, a marble fireplace with a gilded mirror. Velvet and linen sofas, layered cushions and throws, rugs, antique wood furniture, framed paintings, books, fresh flowers, plants. Table and floor lamps glowing. Kitchens in wood, stone and copper. Bathrooms in marble and brass. Beds in layered linen. Hallways with a console. Luxury in the details, never in the size.

WHEN A LINE NAMES A MISTAKE, show that one mistake inside an otherwise warm, furnished room. Never a dingy, empty or unfinished space.

PALETTE: cream, ivory, ochre, terracotta, caramel, cognac, olive, rust, deep red, walnut, oak, aged brass.

LIGHT: golden late-afternoon sun and warm lamplight. Glowing, never gloomy.

KEEP OUT: châteaux, mansions, villas. Large American suburban homes — ceiling fans, open-plan island kitchens, walk-in dressing rooms. Cold grey or all-white rooms. Trend décor: arched mirrors, travertine tables, bouclé chairs. Flat overhead light. Any text, captions, numbers or graphics.

TONE: inviting, warm, quietly elegant — a home you want to walk into.
