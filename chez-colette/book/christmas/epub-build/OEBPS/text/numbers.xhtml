All of these are working figures from the trade and from the people who make the things, not laws. Where a maker’s label says something different — about how many strands may be joined, about a stand, about a candle — the label wins.

The audit · December

The Christmas audit

Room
Date, after dark

Before anything goes up

  • Half of what is on every surface is in the swap box
  • The tree has a floor of its own, a metre from any heat, beside a window, near a socket
  • Every walkway still clears a full basket
  • Every strand tested, one batch, warm white, steady setting, on a timer

Sitting where you will sit on Christmas Eve

  • The ceiling light is off, and the room is still lit at three heights
  • The tree glows from inside; I cannot see a lead
  • I can see the tree twice — in a mirror or in the black window
  • There are no more than three colours, and one metal
  • At least ten things on the tree did not come out of a box of fifty
  • Something living, something wooden, something cloth and something glass or clay are all in view
  • No flame is within thirty centimetres of anything that can burn
Score, out of eleven
