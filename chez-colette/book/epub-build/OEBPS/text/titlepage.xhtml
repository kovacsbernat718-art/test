Disclaimer

This book is educational. It describes principles of light, colour, proportion and material, and how to apply them to an ordinary home. It is not professional advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified tradesperson.

Nothing in this book asks you to carry out electrical, gas, plumbing or structural work. Where a correction touches any of those, you are told to stop and call someone licensed — and you should.

Some corrections involve drilling, ladders, and hand tools. Work within your own competence. If you rent, read your tenancy agreement before you make a permanent change to anything; a chapter of this book is devoted entirely to the corrections that need no permission.

If you suspect damp, mould, lead paint or asbestos in your home, this book will not help you with it and does not try to. Those are surveyors’ and specialists’ work, and the reasons are explained where they come up.

Every measurement is given in both metric and imperial. Where a price is mentioned it is an order of magnitude, not a quotation.

A note on the pictures

This book is drawn, not photographed. Every diagram in it is a line drawing made for the page, because a photograph of somebody else’s beautiful room teaches you nothing about your own. Any room described in an example is an example, written to make a principle concrete. None of them is a real client.
