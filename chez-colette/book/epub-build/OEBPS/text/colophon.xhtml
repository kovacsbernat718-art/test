La Belle Maison
Make any room look expensive without buying anything new

Written by Colette Dubois.
Published by Chez Colette.
Second edition — two hundred and twenty-eight pages,
with the Christmas pages.

Set in Bodoni Moda and Spectral.
Drawn, not photographed — every diagram made for the page.

For Mémé Suzanne, who measured everything
and wrote it all down.

We do not decorate a room, ma chère. We correct it.
