How to use it

Read Part One first. It is short and everything else depends on it, because it teaches you what to look at. After that the book is a reference and you may take it in any order you like.

If you want to do something tonight, turn to The Room Audit on page 181. It is one page, it takes ten minutes, and it will tell you which part of the book to open.

If you are mid-job and just need a number — how high to hang a picture, what size rug a sofa needs — go to The Measurements on page 165, where every figure in the book is collected.
