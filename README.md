## The short version

**The avatar:** Colette Dubois. Channel *Chez Colette*. From the Croix-Rousse in Lyon, the silk
weavers' district where the ceilings are four metres high. Her grandmother made curtains for that
city for forty-one years and kept a notebook. Her father mixed his own paint. Fourteen years
decorating small rented apartments, which she left because the trade's only verb is *buy*.

**Her thesis:** a room is not decorated, it is corrected.

**The opportunity:** The Japanese Method went 0 → 96K subs and 10.2M views in four months with this
exact model plus a $47 ebook. Geo Envy has published Italian, Norwegian, Swedish and Spanish home
videos that all broke out — 9.9x, 8.9x, 5.6x. **There is no French one, and Geo Envy has no persona
and no product, which is why it is stuck at 8K subs.** That seat is open.

**Niche economics:** cohort median RPM $7.30, median 59,154 views per video. Every comparable
channel sells one ebook at $40–47 from its own domain.
